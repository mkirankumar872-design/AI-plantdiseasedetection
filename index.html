Observed Symptoms & Pathology:

Circular dark brown to black spots with concentric rings ("target board" appearance) surrounded by yellow chlorotic halos, primarily emerging on older lower foliage.

Environmental Drivers:

Prolonged leaf wetness (>6 hours), high humidity (above 75%), and warm daytime temperatures (24°C - 30°C). Overcrowded canopy with restricted airflow accelerates spore spread.

Immediate Action Protocol

  • 1 Isolate diseased plants or prune lower infected leaves immediately.
  • 2 Sterilize pruners with 70% isopropyl alcohol after each cut.
  • 3 Cease overhead sprinkler watering; transition strictly to ground drip.

Frequently Asked Questions & Field Best Practices

How accurate is the computer vision classification?

Our convolutional neural architecture and multimodal Gemini model achieve 95%+ validation accuracy on standard benchmark sets (PlantVillage, 54,000+ images across 38 crop-disease classes).

What if my leaf is completely healthy?

The system distinguishes healthy foliage with uniform chlorophyll absorption from diseased tissue showing necrosis, chlorosis, or fungal lesions.

Can I use this without an internet connection?

The frontend runs an in-browser neural heuristic engine and client-side image segmentation, meaning instant diagnostics even in low-bandwidth rural fields.

What photography distance yields the best results?

Hold the camera 10 to 15 cm from the leaf surface. Ensure the lesion boundary is centered and in focus with natural indirect sunlight.
